# # 📊 E-Commerce Sales Performance Dashboard

![Dashboard Preview](https://github.com/Annozo-data/E-Commerce_Sales-Performance-Dashboard/blob/4c2a44e99aef704e813030fbcb4b2c70ea1badf5/Dashboard_PJ4.jpeg)


## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the **DecodeLabs Data Analytics Internship – Week 4 (Data Visualization & Storytelling)**.

The objective of this project was to transform raw e-commerce sales data into an interactive Power BI dashboard that provides actionable business insights. The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor sales performance, evaluate customer purchasing behavior, analyze marketing channels, and identify top-performing products.

---

## Business Problem

The company generates sales through multiple products, payment methods, and referral channels. However, decision-makers lacked a centralized view of key performance indicators needed to monitor business performance and make data-driven decisions.

This dashboard was developed to answer questions such as:

* How much revenue has been generated?
* Which products generate the highest revenue?
* Which payment methods are most frequently used?
* Which referral sources drive the most orders?
* How has revenue changed over time?
* Who are the company's most valuable customer segments?

---

## Dataset Information

The dataset contains e-commerce transaction records with the following columns:

* Order ID
* Order Date
* Customer ID
* Product
* Quantity
* Unit Price
* Total Price
* Payment Method
* Shipping Address
* Order Status
* Referral Source
* Coupon Code
* Items in Cart

Dataset Size:

* 1,200 Records
* 14 Columns

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard tracks the following metrics:

* Total Revenue
* Total Orders
* Average Order Value (AOV)
* Total Items Sold
* Highest Order Value
* Unique Customers

---

## Dashboard Features

### Revenue Analysis

* Total revenue generated across all transactions
* Revenue trends over time
* Identification of peak sales periods

### Product Performance

* Revenue generated by each product
* Ranking of top-performing products

### Customer Analysis

* Unique customer count
* Customer purchasing activity

### Payment Method Analysis

* Distribution of orders across payment methods
* Most preferred payment channel

### Referral Source Analysis

* Comparison of sales generated through different referral channels
* Identification of the highest-performing marketing source

### Order Status Analysis

* Evaluation of order distribution across different fulfillment statuses to assess operational performance and customer order outcomes.
  

### Interactive Filters

Users can dynamically filter the dashboard by:

* Product
* Customer ID
* Shipping Address

---

## Key Insights

* Total revenue generated was approximately **$1.26M**.
* The average order value was approximately **$1.05K**.
* Chair was the highest revenue-generating product.
* Online transfer was the most utilized payment method.
* Instagram generated the highest number of referred orders.
* Revenue peaked during the middle of the year before declining in subsequent months.
* Cancelled orders recorded the highest count (250), while delivered orders were the lowest (231), indicating opportunities to improve order fulfillment and reduce order losses
